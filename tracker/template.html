        Method and data

        Six indicators are each scored from −1 to +1 and weighted: RBA direction 25%, home value momentum 25%, unemployment momentum 20%, real cash rate 10%, inflation versus target 10%, GDP growth 10%. Real prices deflate the nominal index by CPI. Scenario paths and priors are analyst judgement; every 10 points of composite below −30 moves 3 percentage points of probability from the early-pivot path to the recession path. Re-run python3 tracker/render.py after adding a row to data/current_2024_2026.csv.

        1988–94 figures are approximate quarterly values reconstructed from RBA, ABS and REIA historical series; treat them as illustrative. 2026 figures are from Cotality's Home Value Index (Jul–Oct 2026), ABS Labour Force (Sep 2026), ABS monthly CPI (Sep 2026) and ABS National Accounts (Jun qtr 2026). Apr–Jun 2026 monthly values are interpolated to match Cotality's −5.2% from-peak reading for September. Sydney 2026 values are built from Cotality's Sydney readings (−1.2% in June, 7.1% below peak in August, more than 10% below peak in October, −7.0% over the year to September) with NSW unemployment from Jobs and Skills Australia; months between those readings are interpolated. Sydney 1988–94 values are approximate, from REIA and NSW Valuer-General median series. Cash rate, CPI and GDP are national in both views.

        This is a scenario tool, not financial advice or a prediction of any particular property's value.
